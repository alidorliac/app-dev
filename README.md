## F1: The Movie

### The film centers around Sonny Hayes, portrayed by Brad Pitt, a former racing prodigy whose career was derailed by a severe crash during the 1993 Spanish Grand Prix. Now living a transient life as a freelance racer, Sonny is haunted by his past injuries and personal failures, including three failed marriages and a gambling addiction. His life takes a turn when he is approached by Ruben Cervantes (played by Javier Bardem), the owner of the struggling APX GP Formula 1 team, which is on the brink of collapse.
Ruben convinces Sonny to return to Formula 1 for one last chance to save the team, which must win one of the remaining races to avoid being sold. Sonny's return to the sport is met with challenges, including competition from the team's ambitious rookie driver, Joshua Pearce (played by Damson Idris). As Sonny navigates the modern F1 landscape, he must confront his past and the fierce competition within his own team.
